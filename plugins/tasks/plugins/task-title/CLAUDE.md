# task-title

Two titles per task: the full title (generated from the description when the
task was filed with a placeholder) and a ≤3-word short title for narrow rows
(`tasks_ext_short_title`, current while its `sourceTitle` equals the title).

- `server/` (portable) — the side-table, the `task-short-titles` collection,
  `synthesiseTitleFallback` (the title a task is filed with) and
  `scheduleTaskTitleUpdate`, which hands the upgrade to the `TaskTitleGenerator`
  host service.
- `machine/` (desktop) — the Haiku call and everything that makes it: the
  generator provider, the `conversationCreated` / `userTurnSent` /
  `tasks.titleChanged` subscribers, the keyed short-title job, and the backfill
  with its warm-up.
- `plugins/filed-titles` — the generator of a host without a model (the
  browser composition): nothing to do, every task keeps its filed title.
