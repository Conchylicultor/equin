# isle/characters

The people of Isle: inked, cel-shaded characters (the hero and five villagers) with a skeletal walk
cycle, ported from the prototype as typed, canvas-free `core` code.

Two halves, so a renderer can cache:

- **Decide** — `characterFrame(state)` is pure and deterministic. From a `CharacterState` (look, facing,
  walking, clock `t`, `phase` = px walked) it returns which pose(s) to show: a `CharacterPose`
  (`look`, `view` front/back/side, `mode` walk/idle, `frame`, `blink`, `gaze`), whether to mirror it,
  and while the view is changing a second, fading pose (`from`, alpha 1-k) under the new one (`to`, alpha k, over
  `TURN_SECONDS`). The turn needs memory of the last view: `createTurnTracker().observe(look, facing, t)`
  returns the `TurnMemory` to pass back as `state.turn`.
- **Paint** — `paintCharacterPose(g, pose)` draws one pose with the feet at the origin, in world px
  (typed against buildings' `Ctx2D`). The caller scales it and mirrors left-facing side views.
  `paintCharacterShadow(g, look)` is the ground shadow, drawn in the world, not in the sprite.

`poseKey(pose)` is the stable cache key (mirroring excluded). `CHARACTER_BOX` (`{w,h,ox,oy}`, origin =
feet) is what a pose's ink stays inside (tested). `STRIDE`, `WALK_FRAMES`, `IDLE_FRAMES` are the gait constants.

The sprite cache (canvases at 3x, `drawImage` blit) is the renderer's job (phase 2) — not here.
