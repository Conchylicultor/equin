# world-view

## Failures reach Reports

Every failure of the island ends in a crash report, through the existing
channels — nothing here files one itself:

- **Thrown in the pane** (the world's `error` arm, a failed read, a font or
  glyph that would not decode, a failed shape bake): the full pane's
  `PluginErrorBoundary` reports it (`react-boundary`) and shows Retry.
- **A failed claim** is the world's `error` arm (no toast): a placement read is
  claimed once, so a title card left on "Building new houses" would wait
  forever. A 5xx is filed by `reports/endpoint-errors` too.
- **A failed move** rewinds the building (`cancelMove`) and toasts; a 5xx or
  validation 400 is filed by `reports/endpoint-errors`.
- **The engine and the bake workers** throw on the page (the next frame, a
  microtask), filed by `reports/crash`'s window-error listener.
- **A load that never finishes**: `useStageDeadline` gives each loading stage
  one one-shot deadline (`STAGE_DEADLINE_MS`, a minute, counted only while the
  island is on screen and the page visible); past it the stage is thrown as a
  `SubjectError` naming the stage — one report row per stage. It covers what
  no other signal turns into a failure: a glyph read that keeps failing
  (reported once by `reports/resource-errors`, retried by live-state), a
  reader cached on a stale answer, an engine that never draws.
