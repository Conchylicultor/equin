# new-app

Home's New app screen, `/home/new` (`newAppPane`, full-surface over the
launcher). Design: prototype proto-1792811301-yksedx (the `page` variant).

- **One input: the prompt.** The standard `ComposerField` — attach on its
  bar, the registered launch options (`LaunchOptionPills`), and Create at its
  end (⌘↵). The app's name, icon and colour are the agent's decisions, never
  asked here.
- **Prototype → App** — the two steps every new app goes through, under the
  heading; hovering a step explains it. This screen is step 1.
- **Ideas** — while the field is empty, a random hand of five starting ideas
  (`internal/ideas.ts`) and a shuffle that deals ones not yet shown. A chip
  only fills the field.
- **Create** files a task under the Apps category with `newAppPrompt` —
  deliberately minimal ("I want to build a new app. Create a prototype
  first." + the description); `prototypes/CLAUDE.md` and the `create-app`
  skill carry the how — and opens the started conversation (`openMode:
  "root"`, which lands in the Agent Manager). The unsent draft survives a
  reload (`home.new-app` store).
- **← Apps** returns to the launcher (`homeRootPane`).
