# data-views

The story's data-view figures — pictures of a data view on invented data, never
live collections: `<NotionDataView>` (a Notion-like reading list drawn straight
on the page), `<ClaudeBefore>` (Claude's sidebar and Artifacts page, two
partial reimplementations) and `<ClaudeAfter>` (both rebuilt on one data view).
Ported verbatim from the prototype's `claude.js` (`proto-1794207359-w7ez6h`),
where the behaviour was iterated on at length — keep it:

- A feature line (under the Claude figures) lights its twin and shows its
  feature in BOTH surfaces at once: ONE highlight box per surface, drawn over the
  figure in the menu layer, so nothing moves (`App.featSide` / `placeBoxes`).
- A highlighted control scrolled out of sight scrolls its list into view, and
  every list goes back where the reader left it when the pointer leaves the
  lines (`revealScroll`, glided through the auto-scroll primitive).
- A menu never moves once open; a submenu opens right, else left, else under,
  its first item level with the row that opened it (`menus.ts`).

Layout: `internal/styles.ts` (the stylesheet, icons), `data.ts` (invented data,
row renderers), `menus.ts`, `data-view.ts` (the one DataView and the three
surfaces' configs), `app.ts` (a figure). Each figure renders into its host's
shadow root.

Colours: the Claude window (`.cl`) is a picture of claude.ai in its light theme;
its palette is the picture's own, the same in either mode, declared once on
`.cl`. The feature lines and the native reading list read the story palette
(the site's theme).
