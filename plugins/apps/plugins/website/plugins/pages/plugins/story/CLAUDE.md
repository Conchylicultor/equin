# story

The story page — where equin came from: "Building equin", the author's own
account, from eight years at Google Brain to the agent manager that became
equin and the data view reused everywhere.

The page owns its heading (`components/story-opening.tsx`) and renders whatever
is contributed to `WebsiteStory.Section` below it. The story itself is written
as sub-plugins, ported from the prototype `proto-1794207359-w7ez6h` (the design
reference) at its default options — `graphs: few`, `head: profile`, `hl: blue`;
`theme` follows the site (paper is its light mode, night its dark):

- [`essay`](plugins/essay/CLAUDE.md) — the prose and the small scripted figures
  (the friction diagrams, the friction chain, the backlog, the screenshots); the
  one `WebsiteStory.Section` contribution.
- [`skills-graph`](plugins/skills-graph/CLAUDE.md) — the author's skills as a
  program graph.
- [`heads`](plugins/heads/CLAUDE.md) — the two animated head figures (a head
  full of agents; the review desk).
- [`data-views`](plugins/data-views/CLAUDE.md) — the Notion-like reading list and
  the Claude-before / equin-after data-view figures.

Every figure draws in the **story palette** (`core/palette.ts`): six roles
(`--fg`, `--fg2`, `--fg3`, `--line`, `--panel`, `--hl`) mapped onto the site's
theme tokens, so the figures follow the site's mode and never spell a colour of
their own. The one exception is the Claude app's window in `data-views`: a
picture of that app in its light theme, whose palette is the picture's own.

The homepage links here through
[`landing/story-link`](../../../landing/plugins/story-link/CLAUDE.md) ("Curious
how equin came to be?"), and the page answers it under the heading "Building
equin".
