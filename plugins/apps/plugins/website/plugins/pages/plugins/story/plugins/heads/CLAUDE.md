# heads

The story's two head figures, ported verbatim from the prototype's `heads.js`
(`proto-1794207359-w7ez6h`, at `head: profile`):

- `<MindOverload>` — agents crowd in from behind, above and in front; each
  phrase travels into the head and jostles there (the newest loud) until an
  answer travels back out. The head is never clear.
- `<ReviewDesk>` — ideas leave the head as agents working on the floor, come
  back to a line on the desk, and are reviewed one at a time. Its logic is a
  pure model (`deskModel`, no drawing) the drawing listens to.

`internal/kit.ts` is what both share: the head, the robot and its CSS states,
the tweens, the travelling thought. Each figure draws into its host's shadow
root, runs on one rAF loop only while on screen (an `ANIMATION_HOST`), carries
everything across a resize, and gets a still under reduced motion.
