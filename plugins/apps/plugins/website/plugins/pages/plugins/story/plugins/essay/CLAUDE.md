# essay

The story itself, on the story page (`WebsiteStory.Section`): one essay in five
chapters, ported from the prototype `proto-1794207359-w7ez6h` (text verbatim,
at its default `graphs: few` — the figures the story needs). The prototype stays
the design reference: port a change from it rather than editing the copy here.

- `components/story-essay.tsx` — the prose (React) and where each figure sits.
  The figures from the sibling plugins (`skills-graph`, `heads`, `data-views`)
  are imported from their barrels; the essay's own small figures are here.
- `internal/figures.ts` — the scripted figures, as the prototype's inline
  script drew them, each owning the box React renders for it from mount on:
  the friction chain (click a friction: it becomes the next row's goal; the
  last goal unwinds the stack row by row), the new friction landing on its line,
  and the backlog (the same tasks pile up and age on the left, are taken,
  deployed and gone on the right). Each runs only while on screen and gets a
  still under reduced motion.
- `components/story-essay.css` — the prose column (720px inside the site's
  measure), the figure card, the arrow lines and the chain's collapse; every
  colour a story palette role.
- `shots/` — the two screenshots; a click opens one whole in the app's image
  viewer (`ImageGallery` + `useImageViewerTrigger`).
