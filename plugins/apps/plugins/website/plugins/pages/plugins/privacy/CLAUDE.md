# privacy

The privacy page at `/about/privacy`, and the consent prompt that links to it.

- **The prompt** (`components/consent-prompt.tsx`, a `Website.Layer`
  contribution): one sentence — "We use cookies to improve our services. Read
  our Privacy Policy." — and the two answers, on a small floating card in the
  bottom-right corner (across the bottom on a phone). Shown while the browser
  has not answered, on every page but this one; never in the crawlable
  snapshot. Design reference: the prototype `proto-1794535780-z2i0h7`
  (`consent: card`).
- **The answers** (`components/consent-answers.tsx`): Reject and Accept, the
  same size and shape — refusing is as easy as accepting. Answering calls
  collect's `setAnalyticsConsent` and records `consent_answer`
  (`answer: accept | reject`).
- **The page** (`components/privacy-policy.tsx`): this browser's choice (where
  the answer is changed), what a visit records, the two ways a return is
  recognised, retention, and where to ask. Linked from the footer's small
  print (`Website.FooterLink`), not from the header.

What accepting does is collect's, not this plugin's: a random key kept in the
browser for 13 months and sent with every pageview and event, so a return is
recognised on any network; rejecting keeps nothing but the answer, and the
visit is counted by the daily IP + browser hash
([`collect`](../../../../../deploy/plugins/analytics/plugins/collect/CLAUDE.md)
§ Consent). The page states those facts in words: a change to what collect
records or how long it keeps it is a change to this page.
