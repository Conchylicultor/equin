# screenshot

The homepage's live demo, in the band where the drawn desktop picture was
(the plugin keeps its old name; its section is labelled Demo): equin itself —
its real server code in a worker, on the workspace demo's data
(`apps/website/demos/workspace`) — as `<DemoScope>` around `<AppEmbed>`,
opening on its home app.

- **Downloaded on demand.** Nothing downloads until the visitor clicks Enter
  equin (`DemoScope`'s gate, drawn by `apps/demo/chrome`'s boot screen); a
  browser that already holds the demo's data starts it at once. While it
  starts, the boot screen's bar shows the worker's progress.
- **Entering grows the frame in place** — out of the reading measure, nearly
  as wide and as tall as the window, the page below moving down and scrolling
  to it; never a layer above the site. Its sizes are measured
  (`useElementSize` on the measure) so the growth animates between lengths.
- **Once it runs**, the row under it offers Maximize (the demo fills the tab's surface, above the site, through a
  `SurfaceOverlay`;
  `apps/demo/chrome`'s Restore strip at its top edge), Collapse / Expand and
  Reset demo.
- The frame's paint follows the site's theme tokens, as every website
  component does; the boot screen's colours are the logo's (`ui/logo`).

`e2e/live-demo.ts` drives a first visit: the gate (nothing of the host
requested before it), the growth, the running demo, Maximize / Restore and
Collapse, with a screenshot of each.
