# layers

"What is equin?" — the homepage's map of the project as three stacked layers
joined by arrows: the technical foundations, the applications built on them
(a shelf: four apps as pictures to try live, every other app as
its launcher tile, "All N apps"), and the vision of one OS-like surface.

Each layer is one click target opening its page (`pages/foundations`,
`pages/apps`). A layer must SAY it can be opened before it is hovered: the round
arrow at the end of its title row is always visible, and the hover lift and the
arrow's step forward (`layers-section.css`) only confirm it. Do not rely on
hover alone — a touch screen never shows it.

A layer's link is a full-bleed button BEHIND its content
(`.website-layer-hit`): the content lets presses through, except on its own
buttons, so the shelf's apps are links of their own above the layer's — a
picture opens the live demo on its app (`tryLivePath`), a tile the gallery
scrolled to that app's card (`appsPane` `{ app }`), "All N apps" the gallery. Never nest a link inside a layer that is itself a button.

The apps are the gallery's catalog (`pages/apps`: `APPS`, `catalogAppIcon`),
not a copy: the homepage and the gallery cannot list different apps. Each tile
is the app's own launcher tile (`apps-core/app-icon` `AppIconTile`), in the
colour its definition declares — the same tile equin itself draws.
