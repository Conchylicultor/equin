Shakshuka for two

25 minutes · one pan · from Grandma's card

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, a pinch of chili
  • 6 ripe tomatoes (or one tin)
  • 4 eggs
  • Feta and coriander to finish

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper in the oil, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and spices; stir for a minute until fragrant.
  3. Add tomatoes, season, simmer 10 minutes until thick.
  4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–6 minutes.
  5. Crumble feta, scatter coriander, serve with sourdough.
