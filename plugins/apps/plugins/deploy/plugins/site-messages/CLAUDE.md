# site-messages

Messages a deployed site's visitors send through its contact form. The split
mirrors `analytics/collect` and `analytics/dashboard`, so a deployed closure
stays free of the deploy UI and of SSH:

- `intake` — ships inside the deployed site: the table, the public endpoint,
  the abuse limits, the notification email, the spam sweep, and the host-only
  list.
- `inbox` — ships in the local app: the site page's Messages tab and the
  Overview's messages card, read over SSH through `apps/deploy/host-query`.

Design: `research/2026-10-24-global-website-contact-form.md`.
