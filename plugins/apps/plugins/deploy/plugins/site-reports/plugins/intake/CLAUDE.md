# intake

The website's public error-report intake, on the deployed box. A site page's
[`channel`](../channel/CLAUDE.md) posts batches here. This plugin knows
nothing about the page, SSH or the deploy UI.

## Bounded at every layer, by construction

Every number is `SITE_REPORT_LIMITS` (`core/internal/limits.ts`).

1. **Body.** `capBodySize(64 KB)`, then a strict zod body: at most 10 entries.
   - `source` must be one of `CLIENT_REPORT_SOURCES`.
   - `message` ≤ 1000 characters, `data` ≤ 4 KB serialized, `path` a pathname,
     `count` 1–1000.
2. **Admission** (`admission.ts`, in memory, fixed one-minute windows).
   - Per visitor, at most 6 batches. The key is `sha256(per-process salt ‖ ip)`,
     never stored (`visitor-key.ts`).
   - Globally, at most 600 batches.
   - The gate tracks at most 10,000 visitors.
   - Over any of these, the answer is `rate-limited` with `retryAfterS`, and
     nothing past the gate is read.
3. **Identity and fan-out.**
   - Each entry goes through its registered `ReportKind`'s own `schema` and
     `fingerprint`, the identity the reports engine gives it. An unknown kind
     or invalid data is dropped.
   - A NEW fingerprint passes the reports **fan-out ceiling**
     (`createFanOutGate` from `reports/server`, the engine's own gate and
     `reportsConfig`).
4. **Aggregation.**
   - Fingerprints are held in memory, at most 500.
   - They are flushed once per window by a one-shot timer as **one upsert per
     fingerprint**: `count += n`.
5. **Rows.**
   - `site_reports` holds at most 2,000 rows. Past that, a new fingerprint is
     dropped. A known one still counts up.
   - Rows unseen for 30 days are swept.

Every refusal is counted in `site_report_drops` per (window, reason), written
in the same flush. Nothing is queued past the bounds.

## Read by the computer as a change stream

`site_reports` is the `site_reports` mirror set (`server/internal/mirror.ts`,
`table-mirror/source`): its change-log triggers log every flush's upserts and
the sweep's deletes, and the deploy app's `site-reports/import` reads that
stream over SSH through its reduce step (never copying rows). The host-only
list (`GET /api/host-only/site-reports`) is no longer read by this computer;
it stays for older releases until the mirror plan's cleanup task retires it.

Only anonymous data is stored: no IP, client id or user agent. The page sends
its pathname and clamps every string (`channel`'s `toSiteReport`).

## Tests

- `server/internal/admission.test.ts`: the gate's bounds.
- `server/internal/load.db.test.ts`: real Postgres, 2,000 simulated visitors
  sending the same and different errors.
  - Admitted batches stay ≤ the global budget.
  - Rows stay ≤ the fan-out ceiling per window and ≤ the row cap.
  - The server runs one statement per fingerprint per flush.
  - Every refused entry appears in the drop counts.
