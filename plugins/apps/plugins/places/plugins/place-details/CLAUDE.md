# place-details

Google's **rich** details of a saved place — photos, the regular week of
opening hours, rating and its count, price level, phone, website, Google's
one-line summary — for the place card (`places-map`). Read when a card opens,
**never stored**.

## Why not a column on `places`

- **Cost.** These are Enterprise-tier Place Details fields (~2× the price of
  the Essentials + Pro snapshot `places-core` stores and refreshes monthly). A
  separate call (`placeRichDetails`, `PLACE_RICH_DETAILS_FIELD_MASK` in
  `places-api`) keeps save and refresh on the cheap mask.
- **Terms.** Google's Maps Platform terms forbid caching Places content beyond
  short-term use (the place id may be kept; coordinates 30 days). Photo names
  expire. So the answer lives in server memory only.

## The day-long memo

`server/internal/details.ts` answers `places.details` (a typed-query live
value keyed by `googlePlaceId`, on-demand, `fixed`) through `createTtlMemo`
(`details-cache.ts`): one Google call per place per 24 h per backend,
concurrent opens collapsed onto one (`packages/inflight`). Kept: `ok`, and
`unavailable` (Google refused the id with 400 / 404). Not kept: a thrown
failure, and `maps-not-configured` (a key set up takes effect at the next
open). A restart empties it. Each fetch publishes one line on the in-memory
`places-details` log channel (ephemeral, like `places-refresh`).

## Open now

Not fetched (`currentOpeningHours` would go stale inside the day): the client
computes it from the week with `openState` (`places-api/core`), in the place's
own clock (`utcOffsetMinutes`).

## Photos

`GET /api/places/photo?name=places/…/photos/…&w=…` (`placePhoto`, a raw
`blob()` handler) proxies one photo's bytes — the Places key stays on the
server, sent as a header. The name must match `PHOTO_NAME_PATTERN`; the width
is clamped to `PHOTO_WIDTH`. Answered with `cache-control: private,
max-age=86400`, so the browser asks once a day per photo and width. 404 when
Google refuses the name (expired), 503 without a key. The card must show each
photo's author (Google's terms): `PlacePhoto.authors`.

## Gallery thumbnails

`GET /api/places/thumbnail?id=<googlePlaceId>` (`placeThumbnail`, raw handler
in `server/internal/thumbnail.ts`) is the Places gallery's cover: the place's
first photo at 480 px, **kept on disk** in the host-global
`cache/places-thumbnails` data dir (`<id>.jpg`; an empty `<id>.none` when
Google has no photo of it, re-asked after 7 days). A miss costs one free call
(`placeFirstPhotoName`, the IDs Only `photos` mask) plus one Place Photo call —
once per place, ever, shared by every checkout; a hit is served with a 30-day
immutable browser cache. Storing photo bytes goes past Google's short-term
caching terms — a deliberate choice for this one-user instance, so a gallery
of hundreds of places does not bill hundreds of photos per view. Concurrent
asks for one place share one fetch (`packages/inflight`).

Cost per card open: at most one Enterprise Place Details call per place per
day, plus one Place Photo call per photo shown.
