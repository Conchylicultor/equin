# accompaniment

The Sonata **Accompaniment** section: one `Sonata.Section` (`id:
"accompaniment"`, player area) — the song's chords played through its groove.
Its body is the groove editor (`RhythmControls`, from `rhythm-controls`);
there is no other way to voice chords (the old block-chord voicing rows and
their global config are gone — register and voice-leading are per hand, per
song).

## Design

- **A fixed composite, not an open set.** The parts are named imports from
  their own barrels (`chord-mode`, `rhythm-controls`); no slot. Each part
  renders no chrome and no gate of its own.
- **On/off only where it means something.** A song with authored chords
  (chord grid, chord sheet) always plays its accompaniment: no switch. A song
  with detected chords (MIDI) has its notes to play instead, so its header
  carries the chord-mode switch (`ChordModeSwitch`, shown while
  `useChordModeAvailable()`): on IS chord mode — the detected chords voiced
  through the groove, the original tracks turned off.
- **This plugin owns the gates.** The body is the groove while the song
  document voices chords (`useHasVoicedChords()`), else a muted line saying
  what the switch does. The section is available for a library song when
  either gate holds — a file document plays the default accompaniment with
  nothing to edit.
