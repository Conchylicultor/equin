# row-fold

Disclosure motion for **flat** lists — where opening a node inserts sibling
rows rather than growing a nested box (that case is collapsible's
`FoldMotion`). Same look, same clock (`FOLD_MS` / `FOLD_EASING`, read from
`collapsible/core`, the one fold timing).

## The renderer owns it; the owner only states its open set

`useRowFold({ items, getKey, openKeys })` is called by the flat renderers —
`VirtualRows`, `DataTable`, the tree's unwindowed `PlainRows` — never by a
disclosure site. The list's owner
passes `openKeys`: the open disclosures its rows were flattened under, in its
own key space (the tree model's expanded ids, a table's open folds; a
`DataTable` adds its groups' open state itself). It is state the owner already
has, so there is no "this change was a toggle" call to forget.

`detectDisclosure` (core) decides: a change of `items` folds only when some
open key flipped that is not itself a row appearing or vanishing (a row
inserted already open is live data). Scrolling a window, a search, a scope,
live inserts, a page arriving never flip the open set, so they land as they
are. A list passing no `openKeys` never folds.

**A reveal may land after its toggle** — a lazy node opens onto a
"Loading…" placeholder its children replace one listing later; a live
DataView section opens empty and its rows arrive with its query. Neither
arrival flips anything, so each toggle leaves an `awaitedReveal`: the lines it
opened (waiting until a change of rows inserts nothing after them) and the
rows it revealed (stand-ins, waiting to be replaced). `detectContinuation`
folds a change that inserts rows straight after an awaited line or in a
stand-in's place — the placeholder folds out as the children fold in. Rows
inserted anywhere else are live data as ever.

## Presence and motion

- **Hidden rows stay drawn** (`withFoldingRows`): put back after the surviving
  row they followed, `inert`, until the tween ends — then one commit drops
  them and cancels every held frame, so the rows below land in place.
- **Each run of revealed (hidden) rows is one growing (shrinking) box**: its
  rows stand still and are uncovered by a bottom `clip-path` inset, fading in;
  every row below moves by the runs above it (`transform: translateY`).
  Offsets and insets are linear in progress, so one easing keeps them in step
  (`fold-plan.ts`). The host carries `hostProps` (an id, not a ref — the
  hosts already hold refs of their own); rows are its direct children carrying
  `data-row-fold` (`rowProps(key)`); rows whose path never crosses the
  viewport are not animated.
- **Clock**: the commit pins the first frame in inline style; the tween starts
  on the next animation frame, re-measured there, with `startTime =
  performance.now()` — collapsible's `startFold`, the one clock `FoldMotion`
  starts on too; a no-op animation on the host is the fold's clock.
  `prefers-reduced-motion`: nothing folds. A new disclosure mid-fold ends the
  one in flight where it is (the hidden rows drop at once).
- **Windowed lists** draw `inFlight` (capped) rows past the window's end
  while a fold runs (`useVirtualRows`' `drawPastEnd`).

Probe: `e2e/row-fold.ts --path /agents/tasks --row <label>` (or `--group
<label> [--group-by <field>]` for a DataView group) toggles twice and reads
two clocks: the real one (what this machine painted — headless under load
paints ~5 frames a second, so expect one or two frames per 200 ms tween) and
the tween's own (every animation the click starts, paused and scrubbed — what
a 60 fps display paints). It passes on the tween clock.
