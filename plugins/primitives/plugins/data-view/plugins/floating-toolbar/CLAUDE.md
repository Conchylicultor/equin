# floating-toolbar

A **toolbar arrangement** for the `data-view` primitive whose band floats over
the view — for a surface whose view is its canvas (a full-screen map). A surface
opts in by passing the value — `<DataView toolbar={floatingToolbar} …>` — and
the host never names it (see the data-view CLAUDE.md, "Toolbar arrangements"
and "Floating chrome").

## What it renders

It declares `overlay: true`, so the host lays the band absolutely over the
surface's top edge (transparent, click-through), makes the DataView root fill
its parent, and renders the view under it. On that band, one `Line` with a
`md` gutter all round:

1. **Tabs card** (top-left) — a `Surface level="overlay"` holding the surface
   `title` (when given) and `switcher.strip` (`EditableViewSwitcher`: every
   view, Add view, View settings). It starts one gutter past the surface's
   `aside` (`ToolbarParts.asideInset`), hugs its chips, and is the one cell that
   gives: past its room the strip scrolls sideways inside the card.
2. An empty `Fill` — click-through, so the view stays reachable between cards.
3. **Tools card** (top-right) — the bordered search field (`/` focuses it, a
   surface-scoped shortcut), the ghost control triggers (filter / sort /
   settings…), consumer `actions`, then the labelled create button.

Each card is wrapped in `Overlay.Interactive`, re-enabling pointer events the
host's band turns off.

The compact fold (`density="compact"` or a band under 360px) is the host's: one
bar on a floating card of its own, past the aside.
