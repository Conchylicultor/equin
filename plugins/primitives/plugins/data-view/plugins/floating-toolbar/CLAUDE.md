# floating-toolbar

A **toolbar arrangement** for the `data-view` primitive whose band floats over
the view — for a surface whose view is its canvas (a full-screen map). A surface
opts in by passing the value — `<DataView toolbar={floatingToolbar} …>` — and
the host never names it (see the data-view CLAUDE.md, "Toolbar arrangements"
and "Floating chrome").

## What it renders

It declares `overlay: true`, so the host lays the band absolutely over the
surface's top edge (transparent, click-through), makes the DataView root fill
its parent, and renders the view under it. On that band it draws the
DataView's own two-pill toolbar — `PillsToolbar` (data-view web barrel) with
`overlay` — so a floating surface and an in-flow one look the same:

1. **Views pill** (top-left) — the surface `title` (when given; over a map
   there is no band to read a bare title on, so it rides the pill) and
   `switcher.strip` (`EditableViewSwitcher`: every view, the always-visible
   `+`, View settings). It starts one `md` gutter past the surface's `aside`
   (`ToolbarParts.asideInset`), hugs its chips, and is the one cell that
   gives: past its room the strip scrolls sideways inside the pill.
2. Click-through slack, so the view stays reachable between the pills.
3. **Tools pill** (top-right) — the search as a quiet well (`forms.search:
   "filled"`; `/` focuses it, a surface-scoped shortcut), the ghost control
   triggers, consumer `actions`, then a divider and the labelled create button.

Each pill is a `Surface level="float"` wrapped in `Overlay.Interactive`,
re-enabling pointer events the host's band turns off. At the surface's
top-right corner the row reserves the global floating bar's safe area
(`useReservesFloatingBar` → `pr-floating-bar-pane anchor-floating-bar-band`).

The compact fold (`density="compact"` or a band under 360px) is the host's:
the same two pills, past the aside.
