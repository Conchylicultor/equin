# swipe-compare

`<SwipeCompare before after mode at? onAtChange? beforeLabel? afterLabel? aspect? chrome? className? />`:
two renderings of one thing compared. `before` / `after` are any nodes — an
`<img>`, an iframe — each laid in a full-bleed layer of its side, so a side
fills whatever box the primitive gives it. No domain knowledge: the
prototype canvas passes two frame screens, before/after screenshots pass two
images.

## Modes

- **`swipe`** — `after` underneath, `before` on top clipped at the divider
  (`clip-path: inset(0 X% 0 0)`). The whole surface is the track: a press
  jumps the divider to the pointer and drags it (pointer capture, so the drag
  survives leaving the box or crossing an iframe); a double-click recentres.
  The surface is focusable and is the `role="separator"`
  (`aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-valuenow` 0–100): ←/→ step 2% (Shift
  10%), Home/End jump to the edges, clamped. A key it answers is
  `preventDefault`ed, so a surface-scoped shortcut on the same key (the
  canvas's ← / → frame selection) yields while the divider has focus.
- **`side`** — both, `smd` (10px) apart, each with its tag.
- **`flip`** — one at a time, `after` first; click, Space or Enter flips
  (`role="button"`, `aria-pressed` = showing after), the tag names the one shown.

## Position: controlled or not

`at` (0 = all after … 1 = all before) makes it controlled: every move is
reported through `onAtChange` (clamped) and the divider stays where `at`
says. Without `at` it keeps its own position, starting at 0.5, and still
reports moves. The prototype canvas keeps `swipeAt` in its reducer and
passes it in.

## Size

`aspect` (width / height) fits the comparison in its parent: the largest box
of that ratio (each side's, side by side) centred in a size container
(`min(100cqw, 100cqh × ratio)`), so the parent must have a definite height.
Without it the primitive fills its parent (`size-full`) — a host that already
sized the box (the canvas's `placeFrames`) passes none.

## Zoom

`zoom` (instead of `aspect`) makes the comparison zoomable in its parent:
`const zoom = useCompareZoom({ width, height })` (the sides' pixel size; key the
holder by the pair so a new one starts fresh), `<SwipeCompare zoom={zoom}>`, and
`<CompareZoomControls zoom={zoom} />` wherever the host draws its toolbar
(`− 62% + | Fit width · Fit height · 1:1`). The geometry is the image viewer's
(`image-viewer/core`: fit floor, zoom ladder, pan clamp, wheel curve), in
`web/internal/zoom-model.ts`; both sides share one view (side by side: each
half is a viewport at the same view).

- Starts at **fit width**, top first (a full-page capture reads from its top);
  a resize re-applies the current fit, keeping the reader's place.
- The **wheel** zooms around the pointer (a trackpad pinch faster); **drag**
  pans when the picture overflows. In swipe a press within 14px of the divider
  (or anywhere, when there is nothing to pan) moves the divider; a click off it
  still jumps it there. In flip a click (no drag) flips.
- The frame is the part of the box the picture covers — the picture's own box
  when it fits, the whole box when it overflows; the divider and tags sit in it.
- Keys on the focused surface: `+` / `−`, `W` fit width, `H` fit height, `0`
  whole picture, `1` actual size (⌘ / Ctrl / Alt left to the browser).

## Chrome

- **`full`** (default) — a framed surface (rounded, ringed, shadowed,
  clipped), a white 2px divider with a 30px round ‹› knob (grows while
  dragged), and Before / After tags (a dark scrim, bottom-left / bottom-right;
  in swipe each fades when the divider comes within 14% of its edge). The
  divider, knob and tags are fixed white / black, not theme tokens: they must
  read against whatever picture is under them.
- **`minimal`** — nothing but the two sides and a 2px hairline divider in the
  foreground colour (the primary while dragged): for a host that draws its
  own frames and names (the prototype canvas — its header band names A and B).

Exhibits: `swipe-compare/images` (swipe), `swipe-compare/side`,
`swipe-compare/flip` — two placeholders at 16:10.
