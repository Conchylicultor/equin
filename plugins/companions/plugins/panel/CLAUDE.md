# companions/panel

The panel every companion draws (see the umbrella's `CLAUDE.md` for what a
companion is). A companion app's layout is `CompanionPanel` over its OWN
section slot — `<CompanionPanel sections={MenuBar.Section} />` — so each
companion orders its sections in that slot's reorder config, and contributors
pick which companions they appear in.

- **`CompanionPanel`** — the whole page: clip → overlay surface → scroll → the
  section stack; no toasts. Host-only behaviour comes in as props, never as a
  host import: `style` (a native window's corner clip), `onContentHeight`
  (window follows content), `onAttention` (icon dot / badge), `children`
  (headless extras: a mount slot, a self-reload).
- **`PanelSection label`** — the one labelled section. A section with a title
  uses it, never its own caption, so titles match across sections and
  companions.
- **`CompanionHeader`** / **`CompanionFooter open`** — contributed by each
  companion to its own slot (the footer wrapped with the host's `open`, or
  `null` for a plain link).
- **`Companion.Status`** — parts of the header's status line, shared by every
  companion.
- **The companion themes** — light and dark, one per window material, so how
  the panel paints is a property of the surface it sits in: `companion`
  (translucent — the menu bar's vibrancy window: light, the panel at 86% so
  the blurred desktop shows through) and `companion-solid` (an opaque window
  with nothing to blur — the Chrome popup: light, the solid `#e2e6ea` the
  translucent fill looks like over the wallpaper). Dark is the same opaque
  `#2b2b2b` in both. Each companion app selects its material's theme in
  `config/ui/theme-engine/@app/<app>/theme.jsonc` and declares
  `colorMode: "system"`.

## Plugin reference

- Description: The panel every companion draws (the menu-bar tray and the browser extension). CompanionPanel is a companion's whole page — the panel filling its window, its body the host's own section slot passed as `sections` (each companion orders its own), showing no toasts; what only the host can do comes in as props (corner clip style, onContentHeight for a window that fits the content, onAttention for an icon dot or badge, headless children). PanelSection is the one labelled section (a caption title, then its body), so section titles match everywhere. CompanionHeader (the logo and a status line joined from every Companion.Status part, pending until each part is known) and CompanionFooter ('Running · <host>', opening equin through the host's `open`, else a plain link). The companion themes: a native menu's look (system font, 13px rows, a 2–16px spacing ramp, the menu palette) in light and dark, one per window material — `companion` (translucent: light, the panel at 86% over a vibrancy window, the menu bar) and `companion-solid` (an opaque window with nothing to blur, the Chrome popup: light, the solid #e2e6ea panel) — selected per companion app, which declares colorMode 'system' so it follows the OS.
